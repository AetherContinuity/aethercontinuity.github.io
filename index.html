Open research program

Continuity is not guaranteed by capacity.

Critical systems rarely fail because capacity runs out. They fail because the moment at which a decision could still have changed the outcome passed before the decision was made.

ACI analyses that failure mode in energy infrastructure, governance and decision-critical computing: 139 open documents, 20 of them working papers, and 4 live instruments, applied to Nordic and small-state operating environments.

ACI diagnoses and publishes. It does not consult, implement or advocate. Introduction →   Method, position and non-goals →

Independent one-person research program, AI-assisted.
No institutional affiliation. No peer review. No external funding.
Evaluate it by the transparency of its methods and the falsifiability of its findings.

Live instruments

WEM
Winter Endurance Monitor
Finland’s power system under cold, low-wind stress, read as five questions: how often, how deep, how long, what corrects it, and where the energy comes from. Live Fingrid and ENTSO-E data.
HEM
Hydrological Endurance Monitor
Nordic reservoir content against the 2015-onward median for the same week (NO, SE, FI), and the Iisvesi–Virmasvesi lake pilot. NVE, SYKE and ENTSO-E.
OGAS3
OGAS3 — Decision-chain monitor
Monthly snapshots of Finnish government bills from preparation to parliamentary votes and statutes, with the lobbying register and EIA procedures. Basis for pre-registered predictions.
BEM
Biodiversity Endurance Monitor
Rautalammin reitti pilot: species observations, land-cover fragmentation and vegetation index. FinBIF, CORINE and Sentinel-2.
